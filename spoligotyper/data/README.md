# Reference data

| File | Content | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `spoligo_spacers.fasta` | The 43 spacer sequences (25 bp) of the standard spoligotyping membrane | Kamerbeek J *et al.* J Clin Microbiol 35:907-914 (1997). https://doi.org/10.1128/jcm.35.4.907-914.1997 |
| `spacer_variants.fasta` | Known variants of standard spacers, counted for their spacer: spacer 3 of *M. orygis* (2 mismatches) | Found in the *M. orygis* genomes and reads of the validation; its patterns are named with spacer 3 present in Mbovis.org (e.g. SB0422) and in Zwyer M *et al.* Open Research Europe 1:100 (2021). https://doi.org/10.12688/openreseurope.14029.2 |
| `spoligotype_db.txt` | 1,976 SB numbers with their octal and binary patterns | Snapshot of the [Mbovis.org](https://www.mbovis.org/) database. Smith NH, Upton P. Infect Genet Evol 12:873-876 (2012). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.meegid.2011.08.002 |
| `markers.fasta` | 100 bp segments of the H37Rv genome (NC_000962.3): MTBC-specific controls, and segments of the regions of difference RD1, RD4, RD7, RD9 and RD12 | Built by `scripts/make_reference_data.py` from public genomes (see the script). Regions of difference: Brosch R *et al.* PNAS 99:3684-3689 (2002). https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.052548299 |
| `lineage_barcode.tsv` | The 62 SNPs of the lineage barcode, lineage names and main spoligotype families | Coll F *et al.* A robust SNP barcode for typing *Mycobacterium tuberculosis* complex strains. Nat Commun 5:4812 (2014). https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms5812. Supplementary Table 3 and Supplementary Data 1, reused under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) license |
| `lineage_snps.fasta` | For each barcode SNP, 61 bp of H37Rv centred on the SNP, with each allele | Built by `scripts/make_reference_data.py` from `lineage_barcode.tsv` and H37Rv |
| `livestock_barcode.tsv` | 88 SNPs marking the livestock-associated MTBC lineages (La1 to La3) and La1 sublineages, with their ancestral and derived alleles | Zwyer M *et al.* A new nomenclature for the livestock-associated *Mycobacterium tuberculosis* complex based on phylogenomics. Open Research Europe 1:100 (2021). https://doi.org/10.12688/openreseurope.14029.2. Extended data Table 4 (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5730685), the SNPs marked "KvarQ_informative" (one left out, see the file), reused under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) license |
| `livestock_snps.fasta` | For each SNP of `livestock_barcode.tsv`, 61 bp of H37Rv centred on the SNP, with the ancestral and the derived allele | Built by `scripts/make_reference_data.py` from `livestock_barcode.tsv` and H37Rv |
| `l1_barcode.tsv` | The 1,835 SNPs of the lineage 1 sublineages (L1.1 to L1.3 and their sublineages), with their ancestral (H37Rv) and derived alleles on the plus strand | Netikul T *et al.* Whole-genome single nucleotide variant phylogenetic analysis of *Mycobacterium tuberculosis* Lineage 1 in endemic regions of Asia and Africa. Sci Rep 12:1565 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-05524-0. Supplementary Table S6 (alleles complemented for genes on the minus strand), reused under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) license |
| `l1_snps.fasta` | For each SNP of `l1_barcode.tsv`, 61 bp of H37Rv centred on the SNP, with the ancestral and the derived allele | Built by `scripts/make_reference_data.py` from `l1_barcode.tsv` and H37Rv |
